# Armadillo TV

A Windows-first IPTV player and DVR: a multi-panel live grid, full program
guide, scheduled recordings and series passes, catch-up, VOD — plus a
companion web app so you can browse, watch, record, cast, and download from
your phone on your home network (or remotely over Tailscale).

**Bring your own sources.** Armadillo TV ships with no content: connect your
own playlist (M3U) or portal login (Xtream Codes), or try the built-in demo
playlist of free, publicly available channels.

## Download

Grab the latest installer from the
[**Releases page**](https://github.com/knarrlyllc/armadillotv-download/releases/latest) —
`ArmadilloTV-Setup-<version>.exe`.

- **Requirements:** Windows 10 1809+ (x64). Everything else is bundled —
  .NET runtime, player engine, ffmpeg; the WebView2 runtime installs on
  demand if missing.
- **Trial:** every install includes a free, full-featured 7-day trial.
- **Upgrading (including from CoyoteTV 0.4.x):** just run the new installer —
  it upgrades in place and keeps all your providers, recordings, favorites,
  and settings. *Armadillo TV is the new name of CoyoteTV.*

## Privacy

Armadillo TV collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.
Your provider credentials never leave your PC. See the privacy policy that
ships with the app.
